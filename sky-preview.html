About this preview

The entire celestial sphere surrounds you, above and below: no horizon. The moon moves and changes phase at natural speed.

Stars gently brighten and dim independently, inspired by atmospheric scintillation. The Moon and five planets have their own astronomical positions; planets stay steady. Reduced motion quiets the twinkle.

In VR: left stick walks, right stick turns in 30° steps. You can also walk physically. No teleportation.

Clearing review only: trigger summons a shooting star; grip summons a bolide in front of you.
