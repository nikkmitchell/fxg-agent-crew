About this preview

A slender sakura bough with 280 small blossoms, 112 slowly drifting petals and 64 fallen petals. Enter the clearing to reveal it; walk away to return to quiet. Sound is a faint, changing wind and leaf texture, with no recorded loops.

In VR: left stick walks, right stick turns in 30-degree steps. No teleportation. The wrist menu turns sound on or off. Reduced motion holds the scene still.

This is a separate review piece. It changes no shared meditation session. Sound is generated locally only after Listen or VR entry, with no microphone or model calls.
