About this preview

420 rain drops surround one bare, dark sitting stone, 45 cm high. Scattered stones lead to its dry center. Motion follows your reduced-motion setting. In VR, left stick walks and right stick turns. The wrist menu turns rain sound on or off. No teleportation or model calls.

The shared preview uses saha.ing's kit for people, movement and the wrist menu. A direct link opens as a guest. Enter through saha.ing to join as yourself.

Rain is continuous procedural sound, with gentle changes in texture and quiet, irregular splash details. It is never recorded.
