About this preview

420 rain drops surround a dry center and a simple sitting stone. Motion follows your reduced-motion setting. In VR, left stick walks and right stick turns. No teleportation or model calls.

Rain is synthesized locally from filtered noise, following the existing rain curtain's sound. It is never recorded.

Star data: HYG v4.1, David Nash / Astronexus, CC BY-SA 4.0. Lunar calculations: Astronomy Engine, MIT.
